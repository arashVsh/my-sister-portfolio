About

A multidisciplinary learner with a passion for science and creativity.

Yasmin Vashagh is a graduate of Farzanegan Amin 2 High School, part of Iran's National Organization for Development of Exceptional Talents. She completed the Experimental Sciences program with a final GPA of 18.33 out of 20.

Her long-term goal is to contribute to biological and biomedical research, especially work that connects scientific discovery with meaningful improvements in human health. Alongside science, she has developed a strong record in educational content creation, painting, violin, and competitive swimming.
